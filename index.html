How it works

  1. 1

    Tell me about the trip

    Fill out a short form, then we talk for 20 minutes about who is going, when, and what you want from it.

    I send a short list of trips that fit, with prices and the trade-offs between them.

    You pay the cruise line or tour company directly. I track the payment dates, documents, and details.
